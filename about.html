About Ajorin

The world isn't one-size-fits-all, so discovering it shouldn't be either.

At Ajorin, we believe every person experiences the world differently. That's why we create personalized recommendations based on who you are—not just where you are.

Inspired by the Yoruba name Anjorin, which conveys the idea of walking a journey together, Ajorin was built on a simple belief: arriving somewhere new should never feel like you're walking alone.

Whether you're traveling across the world, exploring your hometown, or starting fresh in a new city, Ajorin helps you discover places, experiences, and even communities that match your interests and the life you want to build.

Because sometimes the best part of discovering a place isn't just finding where to go—it's finding where you belong.

Every journey starts with one question

Who are you?

From there, we'll help you discover the world, your way.
